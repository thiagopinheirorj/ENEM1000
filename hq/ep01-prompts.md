# HQ · Episódio 1 · Prompts dos quadros

**Como usar:** em cada quadro, anexe as duas folhas de personagem (`ref-thiago.jpg` e
`ref-isa.jpg`) e cole o prompt. Peça **sem texto e sem balões**: os balões são colocados
depois na montagem (IA de imagem costuma errar letras em português).

Início de todo prompt (copie junto):
> Use the attached character sheets as strict reference: keep the same faces, hair, glasses,
> clothes and colors. Clean Brazilian educational comic style, flat colors with soft shading,
> navy blue, cream and gold palette. Square 1:1 panel. No text, no speech balloons, no letters.
> Leave empty space at the top for speech balloons.

**Quadro 1 (capa):** Isa (the girl) in the lower right corner looking at her smartphone; a
heart made of water drips out of the phone screen and runs between her fingers. Cream
background with large empty area at the top-left for the title.

**Quadro 2:** Isa sitting cross-legged on her bed in her bedroom, holding her smartphone,
proud smug smile. The phone screen shows a list of profile icons fading away.

**Quadro 3:** Close on Isa's smartphone during a video call: Thiago (the teacher) appears on the
screen with one eyebrow raised, amused. Isa's face partially visible, thoughtful, finger on chin.

**Quadro 4:** Thiago standing next to a whiteboard, pointing with the blue marker at a drawing of
an ice cube melting into a puddle of water. Explaining, friendly expression.

**Quadro 5:** Isa with arms crossed, skeptical face, in the foreground; Thiago in the background
gesturing with open hand, calm explanation. Classroom background.

**Quadro 6:** Close-up of a handwritten essay sheet on a desk with lines of writing (unreadable
squiggles), Thiago's hand pointing at it with the blue marker. Isa's hand giving thumbs up at
the edge of the panel.

**Quadro 7:** Thiago holding a big golden star, smiling, a small round badge in the corner.
Four small empty icon cards floating around him.

**Quadro 8:** Isa facing the reader, pointing directly at the viewer, playful challenging smile.
Four empty rounded cards arranged in a 2x2 grid in the lower half (for the alternatives).

**Quadro 9:** Isa typing on her smartphone and smiling warmly in the foreground; Thiago in the
background giving a thumbs up. Empty strip at the bottom for the footer.
